About us

A place to do mathematics together

Lemma Club is a student-run math club. We meet every week to solve problems, give talks and prepare for competitions. Anyone curious about math is welcome, whatever their major.

Definition. A lemma is a small result you prove on the way to a bigger one. Lemmas rarely get the spotlight, yet most big theorems rest on a few of them.

That is the idea behind the club: steady progress on hard ideas, made together.

What we do

∀Problem nights

Each week has a theme and a problem set. We work in small groups and share solutions at the end. The first problems are gentle, and the last few are hard.

∃Talks

Students and faculty give short, informal talks on topics they love, from knot theory to the math of shuffling cards. Giving one is a great first talk to put on a CV.

≤Competitions

We practise together for contests like the Putnam, and run our own integration bee each semester.

≈Socials

Game nights, puzzle hunts and end-of-semester parties. Plenty of friendships here started over a problem nobody could crack.

Who can join

Everyone is welcome at LeMMA! You don't have to be a master's student. Just fill out our form and join us at our weekly meetings!

How to get involved

  1. Fill out the form here
  2. Come to a meeting Meet other students, talk about math, make new friends!
  3. Check the calendar Talks, competitions and socials are all on the events page.
