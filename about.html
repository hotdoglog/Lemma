About us

Your math community at Courant

LeMMA is a math club for master's students at NYU Courant (although anyone is welcome to join!). We bring students together every week for talks, casual chats, and fun activities like game nights!

What we do

∀Meetings

We host weekly general meetings at WWH. Come hang out and meet other math students!

∃Talks

Students give short talks on topics they're interested in at the moment. Join us and learn something new.

≈Socials

Game nights, puzzles, parties (and free food!)

Who can join

Everyone is welcome at LeMMA! You don't have to be a master's student. Just fill out our form and join us at our weekly meetings!

How to get involved

  1. Fill out the form Tell us a little bit about yourself here. We're excited to meet you!
  2. Come to a meeting Talk about math, learn something new, make new friends!
  3. Check the calendar Events, talks, and socials are all on the events page.
